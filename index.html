Delete your Good Conscious account

This page is for the Good Conscious app. You can ask for the account to be deleted even if the app is no longer on your phone.

Email a deletion request

Send that email from the Google account you used in the app. The request is finished within 7 days.

What is deleted

The Good Conscious account is deleted. The names, the relationships, and the links saved on that account are deleted. Anything still waiting to be delivered is deleted.

What is not deleted

Your Google account is not deleted. A subscription bought through Google Play is not canceled by this request. Cancel that in the Play Store if you do not want to be charged.

Tasks, notes, and settings were stored only on the phone. They are not kept in the Good Conscious account. Removing the app from the phone removes them.

If the app is still installed

You can also delete the account in the app. Open Good Conscious, go to Settings, then Privacy and Data, then Delete Good Conscious account.
